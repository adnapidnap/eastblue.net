The island name is "Momoiro", not momoroi. It means pink.
The name of the kingdom is "Kamabakka". It's a Japanese pun meaning "Kingdom full of gays"
You don't need "o" in front of kama to mean gay.
okama is official, but kama is a slang to mean okama, and is used regularly in Japan.
あ、あと白ひげの本船モビー・ディック号を監視してた23隻の海軍船が白ひげ一味に全隻沈めら れた。それに よりいつ白ひげが攻めてくるか状況がわからなくなった。

Missing some info here.
All of the 23 battleships that were scouting the Whitebeard main ship Moby Dick were all sunk by the Whitebeard pirates. Because of this, they have no information on when they will be attacking.
ジンベイのシルエット出てきたがフィッシャー・タイガーに似てなくもない…血縁関係があるのか な？
Jinbei's silhouette also came out but he seems to look nothing like fisher tiger. Possibly a blood relative?

This says quite the opposite. It's a double-negative.
似てない = doesn't look like
似てなくもない = you could say it kind of looks like
I know it's confusing, but it's a "not-not" structure. (which isn't really proper)
So the quote says this:
"Jinbei's silhouette was revealed, and it kind of somewhat resemble Fisher Tiger's. Maybe they could they be related?"
Also, the island Nami was sent to is probably spelled "Weatheria", given the context of the island (and Nami's position).

Mihawk's birthday and sign don't match.
Crocus' birthday doesn't match his Gemini sign as was introduced in the main story.
Shiki's pirate flag is Baroque Works.
Heracles appears as both Heracles and Heraclesun in different parts of the book.
Onigumo is listed as having Seimeikikan to use his swords which is fine but doesn't explain the change of his lower body.
Croc's bounty is wrong.
Drake's b-day is wrong.
Happa who gave Usopp the onigiri in W7 is erroneously named 'Yamao'.
and such.

L'anniversaire de Mihawk et son signe astrologique ne correspondent pas.
L'anniversaire de Crocus ne concorde pas avec le signe des Gémeaux, comme dit dans le manga.
Le Jolly Roger de Shiki est celui de la Baroque Works.
Heracles est écrit de 2 différentes façons (Heracles et Heraclesun)
Onigumo utiliserai Seimeikikan (le même pouvoir que Kumadori du CP9) afin d'utiliser ses sabres. Mais ça n'explique pas le changement physique du bas de son corps.
La prime de Crocodile est erronée.
L'anniversaire de Drake est erroné.

Etc.

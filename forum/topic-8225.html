Chapter 693: Die for me
Franky and Buffalo+Baby 5 stare at each other in silence.

Franky in anger, throws the shield (CAPTAIN ROGERS IS THAT YOU?) General Shield Boomerang!
Baby 5: I guess you're not satisfied with a toy eh? Alright... Espada Girl!
Baby 5's upper torso becames a massive scimitar. She still has her eyes, goggles, mouth, and cigar on the blade lol.
Buffalo grabs her, and swings her around spinning using his spin ability, and slices the shield in half.
Baby 5 says if this robot is stopping them from recovering Caesar, then it's clearly an enemy. Her upper torso turns into a bomb, and Buffalo spin-throws her at GF.
Franky fires at her, but takes evasive action to dodge the bullets, and slams herself into GF!

Meanwhile, near the explosion on the tanker... Caesar regains conciousness.

Chapter 752 "Palm" (of the hand)
Cover is Jinbe in a little pod-like syabon boat, coming across a lost sea-kitty.
]Luffy, unable to move on his own, thanks the two for saving them.
Apparently Kelly Funk tricked Luffy, and was about to kill the A&J who were lying on the ground after hitting their head on the entrance. But out of nowhere Doflamingo appeared, slicing and downing Kelly Funk in matter of seconds.
Caven and others have long gone ahead, now Luffy & co are far behind.
Meanwhile Kyros reaches the first level, only to hear "gomu gomu......"
As the coliseum gladiators make their way through the 2nd level, Luffy's Elephant Gun punches through the floor, knocking out the Donquixote family members in front of the gladiators. Luffy has made a short cut.
Don Chinjao takes this chance to go straight to the 3rd floor, as Luffy comes jumping out of the hole he just made.
Meanwhile Robin, Rebecca, and Barto are shocked at Tontatt'as proposition to "fly" using the beetles.
The beetles can't actually fly carrying the humans, but they can "glide" down carrying them on ropes.
The group glides towards Pica statue, and plans to use it as stairs to reach the palace. They head for the 4th level.
Small panels of other scenes:
Tontatta team that reached Franky has a plan to open the Factory gates. They ask Franky to hold off Senior until the plan is executed.
Zoro, (with a good evil smile on his face) clashing swords with Pica. Glad you're having fun, Zoro..
Sabo vs Fujitora, the clash causes massive fire to erupt, engulfing a large portion of the city.
As Luffy heads to 2nd level, Caven comes to Luffy and tells him he has a plan...
Doflamingo side:
Trebol asks if Dofla sent his "string Joker" somewhere
Dofla tells Trebol he had something to deliver, but it seems it was unnecessary, as he plays around with a key in his hand.
He asks his highest officers if they remember when they first met Law. The boy was crazy...
The kid reminded Dofla of his younger days. He knew the boy would become his right-hand man. It's a shame I have to kill him...
Flashback:
A young boy demands to join Doflamingo's pirate crew. He has grenades strapped to his body.
I want to destroy everything. Towns... houses... people... everything! I grew up in the "White Town"... I don't have time left to live.
Present time Doflamingo reminiscence the encounter with a serious look on his face.
End of chapter - break next issue, back in issue 34

"Venom Demon: Hell's Judgement!"

The poison spreads through even stones and wax. Mr.3 took off Luffy's armor in a hurry, before the poison got to him.

It's Ivasan's giant head, that everyone's holding onto. He did a wink to blast out.
He's carrying Inazuma also, so all the main characters in the coverspread made it out.

Chapter title: Momonosuke, the son and heir of Kouzuki clan
Oden was the crewnate of Roger. Robin remembers Rayleigh's words "We know everything about the history".
Doflamingo and Caesar as well as Kaidou were trying to capture Momonosuke and other samurais to know the secret of the world. But the samurais (Momonosuke, Kinemon etc...) know nothing about it.
Oden left a will to open Wano country to the world.
Raizou says they will defeat Shougun and open Wano country. Shougun is allied with Kaidou. They are now controlling most regions of Wano. Inuarashi says Mink tribe will show their "true power" when they confront Kaidou army again.
Kinemon asks Luffy and Law to fight Kaidou and Shougun with them, but Luffy rejects. Luffy says Momonosuke himself should ask about it since he is responsible for the samurais and Minks.
Momonosuke agrees and ask Luffy for help. Both of his parents were killed by Kaidou. Momonosuke tries to show Dogeza, but Luffy stops him.
Finally Luffy decides to form an alliance with them. He declares he will defeat Kaidou. According to Luffy, an alliance means "friends", but Law instantly denies it (funny scene).
Luffy tells them to wait until he brings back Sanji. He says Sanji is strong. Luffy will go to the whereabouts of Big Mom with Pekoms and doesn't intend to fight her.
A party is about to start since Inuarashi and Nekomamushi stopped fighting.
Meanwhile near the foot of Zou, Jack comes back....

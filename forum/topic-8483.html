But Zoro realizes both his face and his arms are nothing but stone statue.
He spots Pica's real body sticking out of the arm, observing them.
Ucy rushes at Pica, carrying Luffy & co.
Pica unsheathes a giant katana and swings at Ucy. Ucy dodges the first attack, and Zoro stops the second swing.
Pica tells Zoro to get out of his way, his business is with the Straw Hat.
Zoro mocks him, telling him his captain has no time to waste with a little rock. Mr Soprano voice.
With Pica out, the giant statue stops moving. Cavendish and others take the chance to rush to the palace.

His original plan was to have Dofla taken out indirectly... but in reality he always wanted to get back at Doflamingo himself.

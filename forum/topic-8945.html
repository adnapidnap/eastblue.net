Chapter title: "Fluffy and Gooey"
(Raw title とろふわ is a Japanese onomatopoeia for delicious-looking foods. It is one of the lyrics Streusen sang several chapters ago.)
The roof of the castle begins to crumble, and a large crack starts to develop. The soldiers erupt in confusion. The many citizens of the capital of Sweet City become mixed up in the destruction of the castle. Sanji and Reiju grab the collapsed Luffy. Judge is carried by Niji and Yonji.
Everyone inside Bege's body is astonished by what's happening outside. The people caught on the slope of the roof are sent tumbling down to the ground. BM is among them.
The only ones safe in the air were Ceasar and Bege.
Bege watches the BM pirates fall and declares in a loud voice that the BM Pirates are now completely annihilated.
The escaping city citizens are rained on by tiles.
Morgans loses control of himself in excitement and starts to take pictures.
Stussy escapes using Geppou.
Ichiji visually confirms that Ceasar escapes to the safe perimeter.
Streusen activates his ability and changes the crumbling castle into a gigantic Whole Cake.
The falling debris changes into sponges and cream, saving their lives.
The BM pirates immediately began their pursuit.
The Straw Hat Pirates and Firetank Pirates assemble at the northwest portion of Whole Cake Island.
Ceasar recieves his heart from Bege.
Vito confirms the BM Pirates survival, and they re-begin their escape.
Just as Katakuri organizes a pursuit team, BM's condition changes.
BM: "There's... cake there... with specially picked ingredients.. The best wedding cake... We...dding...Cake...~"
Big Mom erupts in one of her feeding frenzies.
End

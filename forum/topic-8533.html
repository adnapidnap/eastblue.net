Chapter 765: Island of Destiny Minion
The people of port town tell Jinbe how the ruins suddenly collapsed on the city, the sea monsters sunk the ships, and the offerings all disappeared.
Doflamingo calls Corazon, asks him how they’ve been doing for the past six month.
He asks them if they’re fine, and if they have found a good doctor.
Corazon replies with Morse code taps.
He tells Corazon to bring Law back to the ship, there may be a cure.
Ope Oper fruit has been found… the marines are going to pay a hefty amount of money to buy the fruits from an ignorant bunch of pirates who don’t know the value of the fruit. The WG may be behind it and this is a dangerous gig, but we’re going to snatch it.
The most trust worthy person needs to eat the fruit… Corazon, you eat it. And cure Law’s ailment.
Dofla hangs up the phone and asks his three Executive officers what they think.
Otsuru and other marines have not been pursuing them for the past six month… Corazon may have leaked our intel.
Dofla says he doesn’t want to have doubts of his own brother, but…
Corazon tells Law in joy that Ope Ope could save his life.
But to use the fruit to fullest, the user needs to have medical knowledge… he tells Law to eat the fruit.
Corazon suspects his treason has been found by Dofla, and besides, Dofla is not aware Corazon is already an ability user.
He probably plans to make him eat the fruit, and turn Dofla immortal.
He schemes to snag the fruit for Law, then flee together.
He calls the Marine HQ, which replies “Okaki”, to which Corazon answers “Arare”
(Okaki and Arare are traditional Japanese snacks, like senbei. These are used as code names here)
It seems Sengoku is arguing with Garp, and Garp even calls up Kuzan to come eat senbei with him in the office. lol
Cora tells Sengoku he heard about the Ope Ope fruit deals, to which Sengoku is shocked to hear.
Where did you hear this!? This is top secret stuff!
Cora leaks Dofla’s intent to snatch the fruit, and gains info on when and where the trade is going to happen.
North Blue island of Rubeck.
Cora ends the call, and finds Law suffering. He tells Law to hang in there, just three more weeks and we’ll have the chance to fix you…
Corazon heads to the trading location.
A huge storm in the area slows them down.
Law tells Corazon that the Government knew about the poison, but they let us die for profit.
They are responsible for death of his family and his home town. Tell me honestly, if you are a marine on their side…
Corazon tells Law that he is not a marine!
After stealing Ope Ope we will be enemies of Donquixote family, the Marines, and the World Government.
It will not be an easy life!
3 days before the trade, Cora learns from Sengoku. The pirate who has the fruit is Diez Barrel, and their hideout is located on the Minion island. Barrel is a former marine officer. They have the island under watch, and two ships are watching Doflamingo at the Sparrow island as well. They will not allow Dofla to interfere with the trade.
Barrel is drinking with his pirate crew, mocking the government for offering so much gold for the fruit.
He knows the fruit is legendary for doctors, and any doctor with the power would be highly sought out.
But to them only money is worth anything.
Cora shows off how his power works to law. Even if he breaks a vase, fires a bazooka, or farts, it’s completely silent.
Cora uses this power to surprise attack the base. He sneaks in and defeats Barrel, and takes the fruit.
As he escapes the hideout, he trips and falls on his back. He is surrounded by pirates…
Law suddenly hear gunshots fired, even though it was silent until that point.
He is freezing in the snow, and wonders what has happened…
end of chapter.

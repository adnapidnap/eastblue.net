- Megalo's model comes from a real world giant shark called Megalodon.

Oda says Aokiji used to have "Blazing Justice" in the time of Robin's flashback, but it changed. He also says some big incident happened in the Marines, and he wants to draw it soon.
- X Drake and Bege birthdays, bla bla.
- The reader asks about how receiving the bounties work when you take down a pirate. In short, they'll pay you even if the person is dead, and if you're a pirate yourself, you can't claim a bounty on a pirate, because you would be arrested as well. You only gain reputation by taking a famous pirate down.

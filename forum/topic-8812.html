Chapter title: Germa Kingdom
Carrot says the rabbit knight is pretty strong and he/she is not mink tribe, whose name is Randolph.
Carrot gives Electro to the crane and feels sorry about it. Randolph jumps off from the crane.
Randolph throws a spear toward Nami. The spear sticks into the Giant who appeared in the previous chapter.
Nami tells Luffy, Chopper and Carrot to escape from the forest, but they get lost.
Fake Luffy turns out to be an old woman named Charlotte Brulee, the 8th daughter of Big Mom.
She says, "I feel like ripping apart your beautiful faces."
Scene changes to Sanji.
Reiju: It's harsh for you to say such a thing to your sister who hasn't met you for 13 years.
Sanji: I broke tie with my family, who displays such a vulgar picture.
The picture is about the major incident in which Vinsmoke killed 4 kings in North Blue.
According to Reiju, their father's dream is to conquer North Blue again with their strength.
She recommends Sanji to enjoy as royal blood rather than wasting time in a small pirates like Straw Hats.
10 maids are prepared to obey whatever Sanji orders them.
Maids: Feel free to order us anything, Mr. Prince...
Sanji's hype is skyrocketing, but he tells Reiju that he won't get married.
Vinsmoke Judge, Sanji's father and the king of Germa Kingdom, appears and says to Sanji, "Are you still complaining?"
Judge: My son...
Sanji: Don't call my name easily! Even if I have a father, it ain't you!!
Judge: What the heck did you do to Yonji? He is pretty strong.
Sanji: That simply means I'm stronger than him.
Judge: Step outside. Man should talk with his own fist.

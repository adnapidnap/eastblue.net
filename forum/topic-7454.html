Trouvé un truc intéressant en cherchant "detect corrupt files" sur google : http://forums.animesuki.com/archive/index.php/t-44378.html
J'ai pas lu jusqu'au bout, mais il semblerait que ce soit chaud du slip :
A mp3 file will play when the starting header is okay. From there on even fairly random data, is interpreted as mp3 stream. To illustrate the problem, I will tell you what happened to me once. I ripped a CD on my brothers PC. Then I wanted to transmit the files on my PC, so I can write the files to my mp3 device (my brother did not have the software installed to do this). I started to copy the files, but in the process of transmission, there must have been an error, because two songs were corrupted in a way, that in song A somewhere in the middle, suddenly song B was playing, and in song B its song A that is playing suddenly in the middle of the file.
This example is just meant to illustrate how mp3 files can perfectly fine play to a certain point and then suddenly fail. Mp3 use streamed audio blocks. Each little block is comparable to a stand alone working little music file. If you want to be sure your mp3s are okay, you have to check each and every block of the file by listening to it.

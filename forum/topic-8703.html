Barto and co spots Sunny, and while Barto is excited with joy to see the ship, Luffy calls out for the crew (+ that Gas-dude whose name escapes Luffy's memory)
The Strawhats find no one aboard, so they decide to go on the island. (que Usopp pretending to not go lol)
Barto tells Luffy he and his crew would love to tag along, but don't want to get in the way of the adventure of the gods.
So they tearfully say good bye, and vows to be useful as an underling. He asks for the 4 autographs of the missing crew when they see again, but the Straw Hats and the Samurai pay no attention to him.
Kan draws a terribly drawn rising dragon, (but Robin blushes and secretly thinks it's cute) which the crew will use to get on Zou.
Luffy turns around and says thanks to "Romeo". Barto is in tears of joy that Luffy remembers his name.
The Straw hats and the samurais hop on the back of the dragon, which starts climbing up Zou's leg.
Meanwhile, Vertigo, Revolutionary HQ... Dragon is getting reports of successful uprisings throughout the world.
Donquixote family's fall has affected the world powers greatly, as so many nations were secretly smuggling arms from them.
Dragon tells the men not to let their guard down for these victories.
Cypher Pol has been strengthening its powers in the recent years, we must be cautious.
Koala reports to Dragon that special minerals were found in the weapons they confiscated in Dressrosa.
Sake-Iron (or Booze-Iron) were found in the weapons, and where you can mine these minerals are limited.
They may be able to pin-point who is behind the crafting of these weapons.
Dragon compliments Koala on her work in Dressrosa, and she blushes, humbly crediting Luffy and Sabo for the work.
Dragon asks how was Robin doing, and she tells him how well she was doing, but curious to why Dragon doesn't ask about his son.
It seems he's heard earful about Luffy already from Sabo.
Dragon orders Koala to gather the leaders of Revolutionary Army from all over the world.
Koala is surprised, as they seem to be.. quite a unique individuals.
Meanwhile, on Blackbeard's ships...
Shiryu - 2nd Ship Captain
Lafitte - 5th Ship Captain
Lafitte and Shiryu are getting reports from Burgess. Shiryu laughs at Burgess still being alive.
Burgess tells them he was knocked out for the most part, and that's why his call was delayed.
Lafitte asks him where he is, and Burgess says it's a white... all white island. He hid as a stowaway in a ship.
He tells them to give his findings to Teach.. if he invades this island, he'll be able to get tons of weapons.
Lafitte corrects him to address Teach as Fleet Admiral (Not the Marine Fleet Admiral rank, but the same as Orlumbus, simply an admiral of a fleet)
Burgess also requests to bring Doc Q, as he is near dying without medical assistance.
Shiryu asks him where he is, but Burgess doesn't know the name of the island. He tells them to use his vivre card to find where he is.
What he does know is... Both Dragon and Sabo are here. He has found the HQ of the Revolutionaries!!
BB Pirates vs Revolutionaries incoming!
Meanwhile on Carai Bari island, "Buggy town"
Buggy's mercenary business is booming, with clients constantly calling.
Doflamingo's fall has created a vacant hold in the market, boosting demand for Buggy's business Pirate Dispatch Company "Buggy's Delivery"
Buggy laughs, telling his followers to go forth and plunder on the warzones, as their activities are legal due to Buggy's status as a Warlord.
Mohji reports to Buggy that Hajrudin and 4 giants have quit the company. Buggy is enraged, as they're the most profitable mercinaries in his company.
He demands to know why, and Mohji tells him it's better not to know why, but Buggy orders him to say.
Luffy & co are still climbing Zou's leg.
The dragon seems exhausted, while the Straw Hats chat on. (Robin still blushing thinking how cute the ugly dragon is)
Luffy asks the two samurai why they wanted to go to Zou.
Kin tells Luffy that they owe their life to them, and he should tell them everything.. but he wants to have a peace of mind first.
Kan follow it up saying Zou is where they were headed ever since they left the Wano country.
They need to know if Momo is safe, as well as their comrade Raizou the ninja.
What! Ninja!? He's a NINJA!?
All the boys turn around, with sparkles of excitement (EVEN ZORO AND LAW!)
Robin doesn't get it.

L.Lawliet@ArlongPark a écrit :Just for the sake of completeness:
I had mistakenly skipped one line in my translation of the spoiler.
Chopper is in South Blue.
As for the names of the islands most of them bear some really Oda-ish puns:
Brook's Island "ハラヘッターニャ" - Hara hettaa nya or how it was transcribed "Harahetternia".
As we know that island's people are poor, therefore they don't have much to eat <-> "hara hetta" means "I'm hungry"
Chopper's Island "トリノ" Kingdom - torino kingdom
We get to know it's said to be a country where humans are ruled by birds. Torino can also be put into "tori no" which simply means "bird's" <-> "Bird's Kingdom"
Perona/Zoro's Island "クライガナ" - kuraigana Island
Can also be written as "kurai ga na" which is something like "The dark Island" or "The gloomy Island"
Anyway:
If I knew that there'd be no (english) trans in first place I would have done a complete English translation myself. I'll keep it in mind for the following weeks.

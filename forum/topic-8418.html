As the 20+ contestants left in block D were all knocked out, one person gets up.

Outside the coliseum, Vice Admiral Bastille tells a marine about the kamaitachi of Rommel.
(Kamaitachi is a Japanese folklore monster, and a strange natural phenomenon of wind slicing up objects)
In the Rommel Kingdom, it was said wind blows through at night slicing people up.
The marines went to investigate, and found out the wind was caused by one man... calling himself "Hakuba" (white horse)
The slim, shadowy figure is holding a saber.
The marines have not caught the man, but Hakuba appears at every city a certain pirate visits...
Rebecca's flashback to the fight in block D, as she stares at the fallen Cavendish
Rebecca is dodging Suleiman's attacks, when suddenly Cavendish stumbles onto Suleiman.
There's something wrong with Cavendish... he falls asleep.
Several gladiators take the chance to attack Cavendish... that's when he dissapears
Then, like a gust of wind, everyone in the coliseum gets cut up by an invisible force.... much like how Kuro did back in early One Piece.
It was Cavendish. With an evil face. Rebecca narrowly avoids getting cut, taking the blunt with her helmet.
As the evil face fades away from Cavendish, his actual persona is still asleep.
That's how this all happened...

The Vice Admiral explains to the marine... Caven turns into Hakuba while daydreaming.
Hakuba is Cavendish's other personality.
When Hakuba first commited crime and fled to the sea, Cavendish became a pirate.
The marine feels sorry for Caven, but the VA tells him Caven is enjoying this situation, do not give him mercy.
Cavendish is a genius swordsman to begin with, and Hakuba is twice as strong as Caven.
Unfortunately the fact he falls asleep when Hakuba comes out, worked against him in this tournament format...

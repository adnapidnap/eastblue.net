Résumé de la 2ème nouvelle d'Ace par Sandman d'Arlong park :
Deuce believes if someone wants to be a good captain he should be loved by many people. In this regard, Ace is a well-deserved captain.
He welcomes many crewmates who have no other place to go.
A beard guy tries to attack Ace. He shouts, "Ace! I love you!!". Actually he is a bounty hunter and loves Ace because his head would bring him much money. Deuce kicks him out.
Many bounty hunters rush into Spade Pirate ship. They asked for help that their ship was in trouble. They took advantage of Spade Pirates' kindness.
Ace sighs and attacks them via Fire Fist. "Those rascals don't know good manners...!!"
Ace can now control of Mera Mera ability although he was recently about to burn Deuce's coat accidentally .
Another enemy tries to make a sneak attack at Deuce, but the enemy dropped his sword due to the snipe shot by Miharl.
Miharl says, "Be careful, Deuce-san". He worked as a teacher and wants to help kids who can't have enough education. Ace took him out to sea and gives support.
Miharl says, "Rude guests should be educated.. I can't read books calmly with enemies on our ship".
He wears a silk hat and glasses. He is a strong fighter as opposed to his appearance.
Miharl doesn't like outdoor activities. Even when his crewmates go shopping, he is in the ship to read books. This is why his epithet is "Indoor".
Ace's crewmate named Skull praises a bounty hunter who has a pistol with skull decoration. Skull knows the structure of that pistol well and makes it jammed easily.
He takes it from the enemy and decides to display in his room. He has many skull-shaped accessories. Skull is a collector of pirates goods.
He considers himself as a pirates enthusiast, not pirate itself. He loves pirates so much that he went so far as to sneak into pirate ships many times.
He hides his own face by a skull mask. Most pirates thought Skull was no use, but Ace highly evaluates him since Skull has a lot of experience and knowledge about pirates.
He has been appreciating Ace for it.
Deuce, Miharl and Skull are the brain for Spade Pirates. Other crewmates are mostly combatants. They instantly defeat all bounty hunters.
Ace shouts, "Go away right away with your comrades who lost consciousness!!"
A lynx cat threatens them by hissing. He is called Kotatsu (TN: If you image-search on google with "kotatsu cat", you'll see the connection between kotatsu and cats.)
Ace saved him who got injured by poachers' trap. Kotatsu's personality had been coward since he was forced to work in a freak show, but after joining Spade Pirates,
he is getting courageous. His voice except hissing is cute. Kotatsu loves to be with Ace since it is warm around him who ate Mera Mera devil fruit.
All crewmates in Spade Pirates had no other place to go until they met Ace. According to Deuce, Ace judges people by their personality, not races or appearances.
Ace declares a party. He complains to Deuce that the number of bounty hunters who try to kill him is increasing.
Kotatsu notices many warships are approaching. Deuce plans to escape from the warships by leading them to rock reef areas.
A young female marine with a rapier suddenly appears on Spade Pirates ship.
"Your ship is great, isn't it? Sorry to disappoint you, but your journey ends here." There is a burn scar on the back of her hand.
She easily protects herself from the snipe shot from Miharl with her rapier. Deuce steps back and says, "She is a monster..."
Skull instantly notices that she is Isca "the nailing". Scull explains that the epithet is based on her fighting style to
make her opponents full of holes precisely by the rapier like nailing. Ace says, "That's scary" and steps forward. He isn't afraid of her at all despite his words.
Isca says in strong voice, "I'm Second Lieutenant Isca! You're under arrest!" Ace tells her that
he imagined she would be a middle-aged shipwright man with robust body judging from her epithet.
He shows a serious facial expression and makes a joke, "Your name sounds like a bird, don't you?" (TN: Isca means "red crossbill bird" in Japanese.)
Ace: "Can you slash me with such a weapon?" He turns his body into fire.
Isca: I'll make you full of holes if you want!
Ace is surprised at her sword speed. "Captain Ace! Let's support him, guys!" Many crewmates surround her, but they can't hurt her at all.
Isca: Don't move, Fire Fist! Stop futile resistance and surrender to me!
Ace: It means I'm allowed to make effective resistance, right?
Ace easily avoids her attacks. Suddenly, many warships surround Spade pirates ship. Actually Isca came alone to the ship to buy time for them.
Isca says to Ace, "You're done" and aims her rapier at him.
Screaming voices are heard from one of the warships. It means Deuce's plan succeeded. The warship is sinking.
Deuce says, "Honestly, I wish they had given up chasing us before this accident happens..."
Isca sheathes her rapier. Ace asks, "Are you gonna escape?" Isca replies, "Idiot! I'll just go to help my colleagues!"
She jumps into the sea without hesitation. After saving many marines, Isca is about to be drowned due to a big wave.
Ace stares at her for a while and throws a life preserver without saying any words.
Isca: Why do you save me!!
Ace replies bluntly, "Not sure."
Isca: Fire Fist! I'll catch you next time and make you regret that you saved my life!!
After a while, Ace says in a small voice, "She is a nice person..." Deuce complains that such a strong marine like Isca will chase them from now on.
Ace laughs, "But it'd be better than getting chased by those squalid beard guys, right?" Deuce agrees. Ace declares, "Now let's start our party again!"
Journey continues. Spade Pirates are right before New World now. They are getting bigger and consist of 20 crewmates and a lynx cat.
Not only Ace but also other crewmates have become famous among pirates. Ace defeated many famous pirates in a short period of time.
Ace is disappointed that they need to wait for at least 3 days until ship coating is finished, but he looks happy at the news that foods in Sabaody Archipelago are delicious.
Deuce writes down his impression of the fantastic Sabaody sights. His crewmates secretly read it and laughs at his poor writing skill.
Deuce jokingly makes up his mind that he will never mention their names in his adventure diary.
End of summary

3ème nouvelle d'Ace, avec son invitation à rejoindre l'ordre des capitaine corsaire et sa découvert du fluide. Merci à Sandman d'Arlong park:
Spade Pirates stay in Sabaody Archipelago for ship coating. Skull tells the crewmates not to cause any troubles with Celestial Dragons, Marine, bounty hunters and such, but Ace is already obsessed with the idea about what he should eat in Sabaody including Grand Line Manju (traditional Japanese confection). Deuce decides to pay attention to Ace since he might burn Celestial Dragons.
Female Marine lieutenant Isca appears out of Spade Pirates ship and shouts Ace to come out of the ship. Isca and Spade Pirates have met so many times ever since they entered Grand Line. Spade Pirates seems a bit disgusted rather than being afraid of her since Ace has been handling her easily.
Ace asks her friendly, "Hey, Isca! What are you doing here? Sightseeing?"
Isca: I came here to arrest you, idiot!
Ace: I see. By the way, do you know any good souvenirs other than Grand Line Manju?
Isca: I bought Grand Line Senbei (a type of Japanese rice crackers) for my subordinates since it stays fresh for a long period of time..... No, I didn't come here for sightseeing!
Contrary to her words, she wears plain clothes like tourists instead of Marine coat today. Deuce considers Isca is a nice and honest person although she is a bit careless. Isca keeps shouting to let Ace come out of the ship, but actually he has already escaped to eat foods in downtown. Miharl notices Ace's wallet being left on the ship. Deuce is shocked and goes out of the ship to find Ace.
Deuce sees many mad restaurant staffs on a street since Ace didn't pay money to their foods. Finally he finds Ace sleeping on a bench near Ferris wheel at Sabaody Park. Deuce gives him a chop on his head to wake him up. Ace decides to ride on the Ferris wheel with Deuce since Deuce seems fascinated with it.
Right after they ride, Isca rushes into their gondola. Ace and Isca start a quarrel as usual. Deuce notices the gondola being locked. After a while, Isca keeps glaring at them without saying any words, but Ace is enjoying the great view from the window. Deuce feels stressed as if he is in a prison. He is wondering, "Why the heck are we riding on a Ferris wheel with Marine!?"
Deuce stares at a burn scar on Isca's hand. She tells him that her village was burned by pirates and lost her parents when she was a child. She says Lieutenant Commander named Draw/Drow saved her from the fire. She has heard that he is now Vice-Admiral. Isca joined Marine since she had been admiring him. Isca smiles, "I don't want any children to feel the same pain as me. I'll capture all evil pirates." Deuce thinks it is the first time to see her calm facial expression like this.
Isca suddenly suggests Ace to quit a pirate since she can't believe he is evil no matter how she tried. She tells him that she would recommend Spade Pirates to Marine if they have no other place to go. Deuce can't say any words due to surprise. Ace keeps silent.
Ace notices the door in the gondola being open by his mistake. He decides to get out of the gondola, saying he is hungry. Isca tells him that she hasn't received his answer yet. "Quitting pirates and becoming Marine... I can't do that." Ace shows a gloomy face for only a moment, but soon he shows a bright face as usual. "See you again, Isca." He jumps out of the gondola.
Isca seems a bit disappointed. Deuce asks Isca whether she will take him as a hostage so that Ace will appear in front of Marine. Deuce is pretty sure that Ace is a kind of person who comes to save his crewmates. Isca denies,
"People who belong to justice won't take hostages, idiot!"
She says in a small voice that this is why she wants Ace to become her comrades.
"I'll let you go this time. Today is a holiday for me. I came here for sightseeing."
Deuce replies that he already knew it and left the the Ferris wheel.
Ace and Deuce walk around Sabaody. They enjoy eating foods until ship coating is finished. 3 days after arriving Sabaody, ship coating is going to be finished. Ace and Deuce stray into a slum area. Ace finds some hungry kids and gives them all of his Grand Line Senbeis. They show smiles and leave cherishing senbeis in their hands.
Isca and robust looking male Vice-Admiral Draw appear in front of Ace and Deuce. Isca looks excited.
Isca: Good news, Fire-Fist!
Draw shows a letter to Ace, which says Gorousei recommends him to join Shichibukai. Isca says happily, "Now you don't need to retire from pirate!" However, Ace refuses it without hesitation. "Joining Shichibukai... Nothing doing."
Isca can't help being surprised. Ace says he doesn't like the system of Shichibukai. Draw smiles silently and tears apart the offering paper. Draw says he has been against Shichibukai system, too. He tries to punch Ace. His fist easily gouges the ground where Ace stood. Isca gets upset and asks Draw to stop fighting.
Deuce tells her that their conversation is over now that both of them hate Shichibukai. Draw is glad that Ace refuses the offer in front of him. He thinks he can beat "Fire-Fist" Ace to his heart content. Draw takes off his Marine coat. He has 2 flamethrowers under his arms. "Which is superior between my flamethrowers and your Mera Mera DF?" Deuce shouts, "Let's run away!"
Draw "I won't let you escape!" Strong fire from his weapon burns a building in the slum area. Kids in the buildings scream surrounded by fire.
"What the heck are you doing!?" Ace shouts and tries to save kids, but the fire from Draw is too strong. Thanks to Deuce grappling with Draw's body, Isca manages to rescue kids in the building which is about to collapse. Deuce doesn't release his hold despite the strong fire.
Deuce removes the fuel tank from Draw. Ace punches Draw in the face with Mera Mera DF, but it doesn't hurt him at all. Draw smiles.
He lifts up Ace and tries to choke him off with his hands despite Ace being a Logia DF user.
Ace: Is this so-called Haki...?
Draw: Your happy pirate life ends here. As a person of justice, I kill you.
Ace: I don't want anyone to mention justice who makes kids in danger ...!
Draw: Don't get me wrong. I'm just working to capture pirates. Without you, those kids wouldn't have got involved into the fire.
Ace can't make a rebuttal and shows a gloomy face.
Draw: If there were no pirates in the world, I wouldn't need to set fire to buildings.
Isca is shocked to heart that.
Isca: Vice Admiral.. Tell me you're lying... The criminal who set fire to my village were pirates, right...? Because you saved my life... My parents...
Draw: Don't complain just because a few civilians became victims for our mission. Sacrifice is inevitable for justice.
Isca breaks down crying.
Draw's fuel tank bursts due to Ace's fire. Both Ace and Draw get injured by the explosion.
Deuce: Even if Ace's fire is not effective to you, you can't extinguish his fire named rage with your Haki!!
Ace and Draw duke it out. Draw suffers a damage from Ace's fist.
Deuce: You're underestimating Ace...
Deuce says calmly. He knows Ace is so strong without Mera Mera DF and he gets stronger even during one battle.
Ace is overwhelming Draw now. Draw is out of breath and shows a painful face.
Draw: Impossible! Why..Why the heck are you using Haki?
Ace: Who knows!!
Ace and Draw punch them in face at the same time. Draw smiles for a moment, but he collapses on the ground.
Draw: Damn.. I hate you..
Ace: What a coincidence. So do I..
Deuce is impressed that Ace defeated Vice Admiral. Ace smiles, panting.
Ace: It's not a big deal...
Deuce lends his shoulder to Ace since Ace is so exhausted that his feet are unsteady. Deuce tells him to go back to their ship as soon as possible since Marine will chase them, but Ace can't leave there worrying about Isca.
Isca seems to be in despair. Ace tells her to get on board Spade Pirates ship. He wants her to become a bounty hunter who aims to hunt him on the same ship. He stretches out his hand toward Isca.
Isca wipes away her tears and smiles, "You Idiot, Fire Fist..! There is no way a bounty hunter and a pirate shake hands!"
Ace, Deuce and Isca run out of the slum area. They can escape safely since Isca tells them the whereabouts of Marines. They find Spade Pirates ship gradually departing from a port. Skull tells them to hurry up. They need to jump from the port to their ship.
Ace: Let's go, Isca!
Ace and Deuce jumps to their ship, but Ace is surprised to see Isca still staying at the port.
Ace: Why..!?
Isca: I'm Marine... I can't go with you...
She smiles weakly.
Ace: Why!!
Isca: Don't die, Ace. Thank you. (TN: This is the first time Isca calls his name. She has been calling him "Fire Fist".)
The distance between Ace and Isca is getting bigger as the ship moves forward.
Ace is disappointed. "Should I have shaken hands with her..? But it can't be helped since I'm a pirate and my hands turns into fire."
Ace pulls down his hat over his eyes.
Deuce: Ace...You...
Ace always considers that he should not be loved by others, but Deuce knows Ace is like a sun for everyone. Warships are approaching. Spade Pirates escapes from them thanks to Ace's fire fist.
Ace: Let's go, guys!!
Ace has to fight as a captain no matter what separation he experiences. Spade Pirates ship dives into the deep sea toward Fishman Island. In the dark deep sea, Ace's ambition is burning. He is heading for New World and plans to take Whitebeard's head, who is said to be the nearest person from ONE PIECE. The ship gradually goes into the dark sea.

724 - Law's plan
Sanji kicks Dofla with Premier Hachis, but Doflamingo easily dodges the attack.
He retaliates with five-strings slash that slices up Sanji.
He tells Sanji to try and save his crew, leaving the bloodied Sanji behind to attack Nami and co.
Sanji chase after him and rushes with an attack, which Dofla blocks all.
He compliments Sanji on the strength of the attack, but sticks his hands out and stops Sanji's movement in mid air.
Jora says he's finished, he's been caught in the young master's web.
Dofla stretches out a single thick strand of strings like a rope behind him (much like Luffy's motion when doing bazooka)
Law sees the fight and tosses a tree log in the air, and switches himself and Caesar's places with it.
He then creates a massive ROOM, and snatches Sanji out of harms way just as Dofla lands the attack.
The attack misses, and hits a building far away in Dressrosa, splitting it in half.
Law lands on the Sunny carrying Sanji and Caesar.
Caesar demands Jora to save him, but she replies she's caught as well.
Sanji and Law exchange information with the crew, such as the state of the plan to destroy the Factory, whereabouts of Kin, etc.
Law gives Caesar back his heart (presumably) and tells Nami to take Caesar to Zou.
Nami tells him they can't abandon their captain, but just as she said so a flying marine battleship arrives.
And from the other direction, Doflamingo arrives.
The ship fires cannons at Sunny, just as Fujitora summons a massive meteorite.
Law creates a large ROOM and slams the meteorite into the ship.
Nami and co decides to follow the orders and head for Zou (they want to get out of this place ASAP lol)
Sanji tells Law Dressrosa is supposed to be just a means to and end, and that is Kaidou's head.
He points out Law seems to stubbornly want to go after Doflamingo.
Fujitora decides to change the direction of the ship to Dressrosa instead of pursuing Sunny.
Dofla attacks Sunny with that rope attack again, but Law stops it, wrapping the rope around his sword.
--- Update From New Post Merge ---
He tells the crew to find where there aren't any clouds and sail.
Doflamingo's ability is Ito Ito (String String Fruit). He hooks his strings on clouds to move around.
Without any clouds, he won't be able to pursue.
Chopper readies the Coup Burst.
Law takes Jora as a hostage using his sword, and threatens Dofla.
Seeing Jora in danger, Dofla cringes but stops his attack.
As Sunny bursts away, Law lands on the bridge with Jora still hostage, facing Dofla.
Dofla asks him what is the point of letting just half of Straw Hats escape, and Law tells him his alliance with Straw Hats ends here.
He tells Dofla he's been using the Straw Hats to stop the production of SMILE, that was his sole plan.
Even if he doesn't stop Dofla here, Kaidou will take care of him anyways.
Dofla guesses Law is trying to take him down with him.
He guessed right. Law says he'd like to see the chaos of the world after Joker's death, but to him, taking care of their old business comes first. A feud between them from 13 years ago.

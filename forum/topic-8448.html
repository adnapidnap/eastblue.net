Merci à Aohige_AP d'Arlong Park :
chapter 740 "It's up to you!"
coverart is Chopper dancing hula with squirrel and an ape
Luffy and Violet runs through the B1F of the palace.
The walls are constantly shifting, and every way is a deadend, thanks to Pica's ability.
Violet worries about Zoro they left behind, but Luffy says he'll be alright no matter what
Zoro vs Pica
Pica is no longer shape of a man, he's stretched across from wall to wall, with a face in the middle.
Zoro has no idea how to end this fight, every cut he makes seems to have no effect on Pica.
(Very logia-like)
Meanwhile in 1F, the toy soldier, Kabu, and Rambo make their way through, but Gladius stands in their way.
Kabu and Rambo decides to attack him from two opposite sides to buy the soldier time to run, but Gladius easily catches the two Tontattas.
His arms blow up big like baloon, then explodes, knocking out both Tontattas.
Gladius is a Pam Pam fruit user, and is a Puncture man.
(this will be tricky to translate, "punc" in Japanese is short for tire punctures, but it's not the English word puncture... it means inflated to the point of bursting. So more accurately he's a bursting-man)
He can inflate any non-organic substance and explode it.
So he's basically DIG DUG MAN
He grabs the toy soldier and inflates his head big. Right as it's about to burst, Luffy arrives and Jet stamps Gladius away.
Gladius is pissed seeing Violet with the Strawhat, he cannot forgive traitors.
Although she insists she's not a traitor, as she was never aligned with them in the first place.
She runs with Luffy, as they have no time to deal with Gladius.
Gladius blows up his helmet, sending shards at the two, and injuring Violet.
Luffy decides to make a shortcut, and carries both the soldier and Violet and jumps out of the window, using his rubber ability to head straight for the destination.
As Gladius wonders where they dissipated off to, Doflamingo appears.
Uh, no wait.
Donquixote Doflaemon appears.
The strangely samurai Dofla tells Gladius he'll leave this up to him and head to the underground for Kanjurou
Gladius tells Doflaemon the samurai dissappeared at the Scrapyard, to Doflaemon's surprise.
(Doflaemon does a great job looking like Dofla to be honest lol)
Meanwhile outside the Suit room, Luffy and co are listening to the conversation in the room.
Luffy wants to bust in and fight, but Violet insists on staying with the soldier's plan
Dofla is interrogating Law, very annoyed about being ignorant of what's going on.
Law's plan should have been just the factory... so how does the Tontatta fit in? How is it that Strawhats are working with them?
How did they get into the underground? Why are they after Sugar? If this isn't coincidences, that means they know the darkness at the center of this country...
Baby 5 hits Law, demanding him to answer Dofla's questions. Law glares back at her menacingly, which sends her crying to Buffalo
Law has no idea what Dofla is saying, and tells him the alliance with Straw Hats are over with.
Dofla asks King Riku if he knows anything, to which the king remains silent.
The toy soldier is glad to see his king alive, remembers when he was recruited by the king when he was Kyros.
He vows to save him. He wonders what's taking Leo so long, the moment the toys turn human is the perfect time to strike Dofla.
Leo and Usoland are his only hope!
Luffy: Can I go in now?
Violet: NO!!
There's no where to run Franky, give yourself in! Shouts the marine.
Franky tells him he's going to rampage here for absolutely no reason.
In truth, Franky knows going to the factory right now while marines are on his tail would hinder the plan.
He decides to fight them here, and leave it all up to Usopp.
Meanwhile, Robin has turned into a toy doll.
She tried to throw the Tatababasco into Sugar's mouth using the Hana Hana ability, but Sugar grabbed Robin's clone arm and turned her into a toy. She wonders where Usopp is, he is their only hope left.
The Tontattas are all defeated by Sugar and Trebol. Sugar wonders if the grape they kept trying to force into her mouth is a poison.
Leo is down on the ground, beaten, and remembers what Usopp said about saving them. He leaves it all up to Usopp.
While everyone's counting on Usopp....
Usopp runs from the scene, vowing to never forget Tontatta's bravery and one day he'll write a picture book about them.
But he can't die here now over a lie of being a hero. Besides, it's Franky fault, why isn't he here!?
Usopp the coward runs away...
(Editor note: Don't run, Usopp!!)
End of Chapter

Et donc Oda a laché une bonne info lors de ce one piece ten à la thailandaise :
Oda's interview with ChinaTimes: (Translation: Rio Poneglyph)
After hearing the journalist's guess about OP's ending that 'One Piece = Won Peace', meaning the treasure Luffy will finally find is world peace, Oda smiled and said 'Indeed, the name of One Piece does have something to do with the ending of OP, but I cant tell you the exact reason/clue, or it'd be meaningless.' Later, he said 'After 17 yrs, after more and more new characters being created in the story, I couldnt stop inspiration coming into my head. I think I can continue drawing for another decade.
Petit spoil concernant la suite (comme je sais que certains préfère éviter tout spoil de la série, je balise :
And there'll be new nakama, they will show up successively.'
When talking about the creation and setting of Devil Fruit, Oda said it's inspired by Doraemon, all the inspriations come from daily life. E.g. Luffy's Gomu Gomu no Mi was created because I (Oda) was too lazy to move out of my seat to get something, it'd be great if I could elongate my arm. LOL. Oda also said he wants Robin's Hana Hana no Mi the most because he then could have many hands drawing at the same time. Besides, all the devil fruits that are eaten by female characters are relevant to women's daily requirements in real life, e.g. Alvida's Sube Sube no Mi and Miss. Valentine's Kilo Kilo no Mi. Oda goes to bed at 8 am, wakes up at 12 am, four hrs of sleep every day, he said it's really fine.

Message du maître à l'évènement Jump Festa.
D'après une traduction anglaise (merci sandman), Oda confirme que cette fois-ci, on découvrira bien le pays de Wa dans l'année. Il précise qu'effectivement, il avait déjà annoncé la même chose l'année dernière car il pensait que son œuvre pouvait atteindre cette étape en 2017.

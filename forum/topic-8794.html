Small summary of the chapter.
- Chapter 826: "0 to 4".
- In Pellona Mihawk and cover with humandrill.
- Nami and the rest are very happy because they believe they have met with Sanji to see the boy with curly eyebrows peroe this tells them it is not Sanji, his name is Yonji Vinsmoke of Germa 66.
- Sunny's group asks an antidote to help Luffy, Yonji responds that helping people is not their hobby.
- Yonji is hit in the neck by someone and falls into the sea, the woman who attacked him apologizes for having such a miserable brother.
- We have to Reiju Vinsmoke, nicknamed "Posion Pink" of Germa 66.
- Reiju says the Germa 66 can participate in the Reverie although no land like other countries,
- Reiju realizes that the poison that has affected Luffy is so powerful that kills instantly giants if they take it.
- Luffy is lucky because she loves the poison. Luffy kisses Reiju recovering instantly.
- Reiju tells them that the World Government contacted them by Sanji and thanks to that his father could change Sanji Wanted to "Only Alive".
- In the sea, under the Sunny, Aladdin observes the situation.
By the way, regarding the names of the brothers Sanji, are puns with numbers in Japanese, hence the title of the chapter.
If Sanji is 3 (San = 3), would Yonji 4 (Yon = 4) and Reiju 0 (Rei = 0). That is, possibly more Sanji have 2 brothers might be called Ichiji (Ichi = 1) and Niji (Ni = 2) or something XDD.

Bon je vais expliquer ce que j'avais compris à l'époque.
Les ninjas font des "clones". Cependant ça relève plus de l'optique puisqu'ils ne sont pas consistants et ne sont que des réfléxions animées de ce que veux en faire l'utilisateur.
Le Multi-clonage de l'ombre, est une technique interdite, qui permet à chaque clône d'être indépendant, et de faire sa propre technique.
On peut faire un milliers de clônes normaux et un millier de clônes de l'ombre, la différence restera la même.
Naruto, s'il utilisait sa technique normale, sans le multi-clonage de l'ombre, serait alors incapable de faire un pauvre Rasengan vu que le clône utilise du Chakra en même temps que l'original.
Voilà ce que j'ai compris.

Iwanin a écrit :As intrigued as I am by this Hanzou character, Kishi's narrative ploys are starting to get to me.
First we're told about how the Sannin are miraculously powerful and all that.
Then Itachi and Kisame go on about how they couldn't possibly beat Jiraiya - despite the fact Itachi had taken down Orochimaru without breaking a sweat.
Then in Kakashi Gaiden we learn the Sannin weren't as powerful as Kakashi's dad, who's completely irrelevant to the manga, but Kishi had to throw that snipet in for dramatic effect.
Then there's Sasuke, who beats Orochimaru like nothing, claiming he's now "stronger and has nothing more to learn" (from Orochimaru), whose "genius" he mocks - before later claiming he won only because Orochi's health was failing.
Then we have Pain, and somehow we need to establish he's "really powerful" too - so Kishi comes up with a character more powerful than all Sannin *put together* (because just saying he's stronger than each of them individually wouldn't be so dramatic at this point), and says "and Pain beat him, you know, so *he's* in turn more powerful than all Sannin put together."
Woooow (sarcasm). It would be impressive if we'd heard of this Hanzou before - you'd think, the Sannin being as famous as they were, we would have heard about a guy who mopped the floor with them. I guess only "Kages" get any respect.

Whenever he needs to present an opponent as really strong, he'll simply create a completely new character who's shit was smellier than the last "really strong character who was beaten by the guy Konoha's about to face", who, despite this "legendary strength", we've never heard of before.
Gimme a break.
